Privacy Policy – Private PDF Toolkit

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Summary: your files never leave your computer

Private PDF Toolkit is a Chrome extension that processes PDFs and images entirely inside your browser. Your files, their contents and their names are never uploaded, sent to a server, or seen by us or anyone else. We do not collect analytics and do not use cookies, trackers or advertising.

What we do not collect

  • No file contents, file names or document data.
  • No browsing history, and no access to the websites you visit.
  • No analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising identifiers.

What is stored on your device

The extension stores a few small settings in Chrome's local extension storage: whether Pro is unlocked, and a random licence identifier used by our payment provider. These stay on your device and are removed when you uninstall the extension.

Payments (Pro upgrade)

Pro is a one-off £12.99 purchase handled by ExtensionPay, with card payments processed by Stripe. When you pay or check your licence, the extension contacts ExtensionPay with the random licence identifier only. If you buy, ExtensionPay and Stripe receive the email address and payment details you enter on their checkout page. We never see your card details, and no file or document data is ever sent. See ExtensionPay's privacy policy and Stripe's privacy policy.

Permissions

storage: to remember your Pro status on your device. The extension also runs a small script on extensionpay.com pages only, so it can confirm your purchase once you've paid. It has no access to any other website.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent or share any user data, and we do not use or transfer data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose of editing PDFs locally.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
